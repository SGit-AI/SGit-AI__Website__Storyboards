Cast designer

track: characters lab · experiment · pinned to kit v0.1.0 back to the lab

The kit draws a character from a few fields in a cast file: skin, hair, shirt, trousers, an accessory, a height, or one colour for an agent. This page turns each field into a control and redraws the figure as you change it, twice (a second preview at another pose and mood), and writes the character in the cast format, ready to copy into a cast.json. Start from Ines, or press random.
