The panels

  1. 1. Connect the dots backwards: Your path only makes sense looking back. Learn to walk it anyway. (cover; Connect the dots backwards)
  2. 2. Steve Jobs, Stanford, 2005. Zara thinks: “I can't see where any of this goes.” The Guide says: “you can't connect the dots looking forward; you can only connect (scene; Connect the dots backwards, Connect the dots backwards)
  3. 3. His dots, connected backwards. Drops out → Calligraphy → Ten years → The Mac (spectrum; Steve Jobs, Stanford, 12 June 2005)
  4. 4. The edge. The Guide says: “it is the multidisciplinary person who has the edge” (scene; Connect the dots backwards, The edge is multidisciplinary)
  5. 5. How we teach maths. Tomás says: “Just memorise the formula?” The Guide says: “we give the solution away” (scene; Connect the dots backwards, We teach maths as if to make people hate it)
  6. 6. What just happened while writing this book. The Agent says: “Instead of infographics, shall I draw you a cast?” (scene; Connect the dots backwards, Generative AI lets you explore again)
  7. 7. The beauty of it. “you can now discover how you like to work” (Connect the dots backwards) (evidence; Connect the dots backwards, This is about you)
  8. 8. When it feels like too much. Priya says: “It's too much. Where do I even start?” The Guide says: “Let your questions guide you, and learn.” (scene; Connect the dots backwards, This is about you)
  9. 9. Geography no longer decides. The Guide says: “The future is decentralised.” (scene; Connect the dots backwards, Learning is a lifelong skill)
  10. 10. Take it seriously. “Learning is a skill, and a lifelong one.” (punchline; Connect the dots backwards, Learning is a lifelong skill)
  11. 11. You, reading this. The Guide says: “just by reading this book, you are already in a very small minority” (scene; Connect the dots backwards, You are already in a small minority)
  12. 12. Read it on paper, with a pen. The Guide says: “books, and narratives, are still the best way we have to take in information” (scene; Connect the dots backwards, Read it on paper, with a pen)
  13. 13. Trust your own way of thinking. The Guide says: “My thinking is very hyperlinked: I think in graphs.” Zara thinks: “Mine jumps around too.” (scene; Connect the dots backwards, I think in graphs)
  14. 14. From answers to questions (the next article). “Search gave us answers. Now you can ask for the evidence.” (punchline; Connect the dots backwards, From answers to questions)
