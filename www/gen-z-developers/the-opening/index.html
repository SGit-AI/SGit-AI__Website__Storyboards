The panels

  1. 1. You are lucky to be here: The opening of the second edition, in 16 panels: wonder, not fear. (cover; You are lucky to be here)
  2. 2. 2017: the slide that started the first edition. The Guide says: “how many logos do you recognize?” Zara says: “Hardly any.” (scene; Book v1.0.0, Introduction)
  3. 3. Looking back. “it went too technical, too deep, too soon” (punchline; You are lucky to be here, How the first edition started)
  4. 4. The doom. Zara thinks: “Everyone says AI will take my job.” The Guide says: “nobody knows what is going to happen” (scene; You are lucky to be here, You are lucky to be here)
  5. 5. The first message of the book. “ridiculously privileged to be living at this moment in time” (punchline; You are lucky to be here)
  6. 6. The transformations I have lived through. Spectrum → BBS → Internet → Mobile → Cloud → Apps → AI (spectrum; You are lucky to be here, the table)
  7. 7. The only difference. The Guide says: “this one is televised” (scene; You are lucky to be here, The transformations)
  8. 8. We have been here before. “The Nasdaq peaked in March 2000 and had lost almost four fifths of its value by October 2002, and the internet still changed everythi (evidence; You are lucky to be here, The hype, and maybe the crash)
  9. 9. Wonder. Zara says: “Wait. I can build that?” The Agent says: “Want to try it now?” (scene; You are lucky to be here, Wonder)
  10. 10. Not only for the creative ones. “you can now scale the things you are not good at” (You are lucky to be here, Wonder) (evidence; You are lucky to be here, Wonder)
  11. 11. Technology that worked on you. The Guide says: “Gen Z in particular was given a technology stack designed to hook them” (scene; You are lucky to be here, Technology that did not work for you)
  12. 12. My generation could build things. The Guide says: “Now they can build again.” (scene; You are lucky to be here, Technology that did not work for you)
  13. 13. Learning, made for you. Priya says: “I was never good at school.” The Guide says: “The academic model was generic, made for a mythical student” (scene; You are lucky to be here, Learning, made for you)
  14. 14. Become a developer. Zara says: “So I make my own tools?” The Guide says: “craftspeople made their own tools” (scene; You are lucky to be here, Become a developer)
  15. 15. It is still your name. The Agent says: “Shall I just decide for you?” The Guide says: “it is you thinking with generative AI” (scene; You are lucky to be here, Think with it)
  16. 16. The jobs that never scaled, and where on the map you land. Zara says: “Where do I land?” Explorer → Villager → Town planner (spectrum; You are lucky to be here, The jobs that never scaled)
