The panels

  1. 1. Where do you land?: Maps first, names later; then the three kinds of people who build things. (cover; Where do you land?)
  2. 2. A map of what Zara's first job depends on. (We have not named it yet.). (map; Where do you land?, Maps everywhere)
  3. 3. How a map starts. The Guide says: “You start with the user needs” (scene; Where do you land?, Maps everywhere)
  4. 4. The same map, with its names. And everything moves.. (map; Where do you land?, Maps everywhere)
  5. 5. Why I love it. “Wardley Maps gives me inner peace (allowed me to understand why things happen)” (The eleventh shift in Dinis's talk 'Why Wardley Maps changed ho (evidence; Where do you land?, Maps everywhere)
  6. 6. Not just activities. The Guide says: “evolution is everywhere” (scene; Where do you land?, The four stages are not only for activities)
  7. 7. Where do you fit?. Explorer → Villager → Town planner (spectrum; Where do you land?, Where do you fit?)
  8. 8. The explorer. Zara says: “It works! Right, that's boring now. Next.” The Guide says: “Failure is just an experiment, something you learn from.” (scene; Where do you land?, The explorer)
  9. 9. The villager. Priya says: “Does it make my team's day easier? Then ship it.” The Guide says: “The first version just has to work. The second version has to be d (scene; Where do you land?, The villager)
  10. 10. The town planner. Tomás says: “Found it. One missing comma.” The Guide says: “boring is good” (scene; Where do you land?, The town planner)
  11. 11. they know they have done their job right when nothing goes wrong. (punchline; Where do you land?, The town planner)
  12. 12. A centre of gravity, not a box. The Guide says: “what makes you lose sense of time?” (scene; Where do you land?, Where do you fit?)
  13. 13. Different skills, different learning paths. Zara says: “Teach me to prototype.” Priya says: “Teach me to ship.” Tomás says: “Teach me to test.” (scene; Where do you land?, A centre of gravity, not a box)
  14. 14. The question the book asks early, and keeps asking. “Where is your centre of gravity?” (punchline; Where do you land?)
