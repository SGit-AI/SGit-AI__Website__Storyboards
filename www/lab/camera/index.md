# Camera: one world, many beats

> Three of the kit's panels side by side as one world; the camera pans and zooms between beats written as data, and zooms into a laptop whose screen is another panel drawn by the same kit.

*Source: <https://storyboards.sgit.ai/lab/camera/>. A standalone experiment of the Lab, status promising, pinned to api.storyboards.sgit.ai v0.1.0. The page is drawn by code; this file is what it says.*

---

**The question.** Can a storyboard be one wide scene that a camera moves across, and can a scene hold another scene?

## What this taught us

- A viewBox is a camera, and it is enough: pan, zoom and a hold are three numbers and a time, and the sketch stays sharp at 8x because it was never pixels.
- A storyboard can show a storyboard: the laptop's screen is a nested svg the kit drew a different scene into. Any prop with a face can hold a scene.
- The world is three panels because the kit draws panels; a continuous floor hides the seams, a setting per panel does not. A world kind in the data is the honest next step.
- Beats and actions want to be one script: this page moves the camera, the Stage experiment moves the actors, and the same timeline should carry both.
- Holding still is a beat too: without a hold the camera never arrives anywhere.
