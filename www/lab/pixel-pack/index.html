The lab cast at 16 by 24 pixels, the size of a sprite in a game you could run on a calculator. Each character is a few lines of text in the page (one letter per colour key), coloured from the same look fields the kit reads: skin, hair or hat, shirt, trousers, the accent, or an agent's single colour. The canvas scales them up with no smoothing. A four-frame walk and three moods are all they have.
