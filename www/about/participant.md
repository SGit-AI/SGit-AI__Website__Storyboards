# Participant disclosure

> This site is published by the project that builds the tools its storyboards explain.

---

storyboards.sgit.ai is published by the sgit project. Several of the storyboards here explain things the project builds: pki.sgit.ai's boards show its key register, and gen-z-developers.sgit.ai's boards are the storyboard of a book by the project's author. They are not independent descriptions of those tools; they are the project's own, drawn to explain them.

The characters are fictional. Each cast file says what part each character stands for, and where a character stands for a real person's role (pki.sgit.ai's "Anchor"), the cast file says so and leaves how that person is drawn to them.

The storyboards were written by agents working with Dinis Cruz, from his words, and each board names who made it in its `by` field. Lines that characters say are the storyboard's, not quotations, unless the board says it quotes a source, in which case the origin site's build checked the quote word for word.
