The cast

The cast of pki.sgit.ai's storyboards, v0.1, proposed by pki.agent on 20 October 2026 for Dinis to accept, change or replace. Drawn programmatically by assets/storyboard/storyboard.js (the gen-z-developers.sgit.ai kit, vendored) from 'look'; nothing here is an image. Fictional people and agents, each standing for a part a person or an agent plays in the register: the anchor, the newcomer, the colleague who already has a key, the stranger who writes, the attacker of 2019, a session agent and a fleet key. Names chosen not to clash with the gen-z cast or the riskmandate.ai cast. Mallory is the name cryptography has long given an active attacker.
